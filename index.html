How To Upload

1. Tap TURN ON WIFI FOR UPDATE.
2. The app will disconnect. This is expected — Bluetooth has to be released so the WiFi driver has enough memory to start.
3. In Arduino IDE choose Tools → Port → the network port named rv-leveler.
4. Upload as usual.
5. The device reboots itself back to normal with WiFi off, and the app can reconnect.

To exit without uploading: press the wake button in the cupboard, or wait 10 minutes. Either reboots to normal.

A reboot clears the diagnostic log — download the CSV first if you still need it. Calibration is unaffected.

While Sleeping

Every 30 minutes the device wakes briefly, checks if the RV has moved more than 2°, then goes back to sleep if parked. If movement is detected it stays awake and advertises via BLE so your phone can connect.

While Awake — No Phone

Checks angles every 1 minute. If the RV is still moving the sleep timer keeps resetting — the device stays awake for the entire drive. Once parked and angles stabilize the 20 minute countdown begins.

Front / Back Leveling Note

The sensor is mounted near the middle of the trailer. Due to the rubber torsion axle suspension the sensor does not respond to electric tongue jack movement while you are standing outside.

To see accurate front/back readings step inside the trailer — your weight on the floor gives the sensor a true tilt reading. Adjust the tongue jack, step inside to check the reading, repeat until level.

Side to side leveling works normally from anywhere.

Power Use

Phone connected ~190mA
Awake — no phone ~150mA
Deep sleep ~0.02mA

Troubleshooting

If the device does not appear when scanning press the physical button in the upper cupboard beside the radio on the passenger side to wake it immediately.

If the app shows connected but no data is updating tap Disconnect then Connect again.
